RedEdit logic editor prototype

REDCap logic with highlighting, completion and checks, against a made-up longitudinal mood study. Type [ for fields, press Ctrl+Space anywhere for suggestions.


      
      
    
    
    
    
    
    
    Suggestions appear as you type. Press Control Space to ask for them, arrow keys to choose, Enter or Tab to accept, Escape to close. Problems are listed after the editor.
